Islamic Calendar

About the Islamic Calendar

The Islamic calendar is a lunar calendar consisting of 12 months in a year of 354 or 355 days. It is used to determine the proper days of Islamic holidays and rituals, such as the annual period of fasting and the proper time for the pilgrimage to Mecca.

The calendar is based on the Hijra (migration) of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE. The months are determined by the sighting of the new moon.

The 12 months are: Muharram, Safar, Rabi' al-Awwal, Rabi' al-Thani, Jumada al-Awwal, Jumada al-Thani, Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal, Dhu al-Qi'dah, and Dhu al-Hijjah.
